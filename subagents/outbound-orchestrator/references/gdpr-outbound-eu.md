## TL;DR — Compliance flow per `/outbound-orchestrator`

1. **Auto-detect EU**: ICP description contiene `EU/Europa/Italia/EMEA/{country EU}` → `gdpr.mode_active = true`
2. **LIA mandatory pre-send**: documento `<memory>/lia_<campaign>_<date>.md` con 3 step (interest, necessity, balance)
3. **Footer bilingue IT+EN** se EU detected, mandatory in ogni email
4. **Suppression list cross-stack**: SmartLead + HeyReach + CRM Attio + `<memory>/suppression.csv`
5. **Retention 12 mesi** post-contact, reset al re-engagement
6. **Article 9 reject**: schema validation NO health/race/religion/political/sexual/biometric
7. **B2C personal email reject**: gmail/yahoo/hotmail/libero/personal → skip se `reject_b2c_personal_email = true`
8. **Italy Garante specifics**: Privacy Policy linked + source documented + minimization

## 1. GDPR Recital 47 — Direct Marketing legitimate interest

> "The processing of personal data for direct marketing purposes may be regarded as carried out for a legitimate interest."

Cold email B2B in EU è **legittimo** se:
- LIA documentato (3 step balancing test)
- Opt-out chiaro e processato in <30d
- Data minimization (solo nome + email + role + company, no PII extra)
- No Article 9 sensitive data
- Source documented (come hai trovato il contatto)

**Non è permesso**:
- B2C marketing senza consent (ePrivacy Directive)
- Health/race/religion/political/sexual/biometric data ever
- Email forwarded/scraped da fonti illegittime
- Bulk send senza LIA campaign-specific

## 2. LIA template B2B cold email

Ogni campagna richiede LIA prima di send. Template `lia_<campaign>_<date>.md`:

```markdown
---
campaign: "<campaign_name>"
date: 2026-04-30
icp: "<icp_description>"
agent: outbound-orchestrator
status: documented
---

# Legitimate Interest Assessment — <campaign_name>

## 1. Identify legitimate interest

**Cosa**: B2B targeted outreach to <icp_description>
**Perché**: <business_purpose, es. "promote GTM Engineering audit service to Series A SaaS founders">
**Beneficio**: <expected_outcome, es. "5-10 demo booked, 1-2 deal closed">

## 2. Necessity test

**Domanda**: l'email è necessaria/proporzionata vs alternative?

- Alternative considerate:
  - LinkedIn DM only → meno efficace per role-based
  - Phone call → friction maggiore, ROI lower
  - Inbound only → timing slower, miss high-intent
- **Conclusione**: email B2B targeted è proporzionata.

## 3. Balance test (data subject rights vs sender interest)

**Recipient profile**: <es. "VP Marketing/Founder Series A SaaS USA + EU, professional email">
**Privacy expectation**: low (professional contact, public LinkedIn profile, business email)
**Mitigations**:
- Solo professional email (no personal gmail/yahoo)
- Opt-out 1-click in footer
- Suppression cross-stack post-opt-out
- Source transparency: "fonte: LinkedIn pubblico"
- Content relevant to professional role
- No Article 9 sensitive data
- Retention 12 mesi post-contact
- No data sharing third party

**Conclusione balance**: legitimate interest **prevale** sui right of data subject. Mitigations sufficienti.

## Sign-off

**Documented by**: <user_name>
**Signed**: 2026-04-30
**Review**: trimestrale
```

## 3. Footer bilingue mandatory (IT + EN se EU detected)

Skill `gdpr-opt-out` genera footer HTML. Inject in ogni email step.

```html
<p style="font-size: 11px; color: #888; line-height: 1.4; margin-top: 24px; padding-top: 12px; border-top: 1px solid #ddd;">
<strong>Italiano</strong>: Stai ricevendo questa email in qualità di {role} presso {company} — fonte: {source}. Trattiamo i tuoi dati per legittimo interesse (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR + Recital 47). Se non desideri ricevere ulteriori comunicazioni, <a href="{unsubscribe_url}" style="color: #888;">disiscriviti qui</a>. Inviato da {sender_name} · {sender_address} · <a href="{privacy_url}" style="color: #888;">Privacy Policy</a>.
<br><br>
<strong>English</strong>: You are receiving this email as {role} at {company} — source: {source}. We process your data on legitimate interest basis (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR + Recital 47). To opt out, <a href="{unsubscribe_url}" style="color: #888;">unsubscribe here</a>. Sent by {sender_name} · {sender_address} · <a href="{privacy_url}" style="color: #888;">Privacy Policy</a>.
</p>
```

Variables required in template:
- `{unsubscribe_url}` — auto-generated by SmartLead/HeyReach, MANDATORY
- `{role}`, `{company}` — from lead enriched
- `{source}` — from lead `_meta.source` (es. "LinkedIn", "Conference attendee list", "BuiltWith")
- `{sender_name}`, `{sender_address}`, `{privacy_url}` — from `<memory>/config.md` brand section

## 5. Italy Garante Privacy specifics

### Status 2026

Garante Privacy Italia (autorità data protection) ha emanato:

- **FAQ direct marketing 2020+**: B2B legitimate interest valid se LIA solido
- **Provv. n. 230/2020**: chiarimento ruolo legitimate interest art. 6(1)(f) per B2B
- **DLA Piper Privacy Matters luglio 2025**: trend "double opt-in mandatory" SOLO per B2C email marketing. B2B contacts professionali NON impattati.

### Implicazioni operative

| Caso | OK in Italia? | Note |
|------|---------------|------|
| Cold email a `cmo@company.com` (corporate domain) | ✅ Sì | LIA + opt-out + footer bilingue mandatory |
| Cold email a `mario.rossi@company.it` (corporate IT domain) | ✅ Sì | Idem |
| Cold email a `mario.rossi@gmail.com` (personal email) | ❌ No (default) | Reject con `reject_b2c_personal_email: true` |
| Cold email a founder con email `f@founder.io` (boutique consulting) | ⚠️ Borderline | Manual review se domain matches LinkedIn company |
| Cold email a `info@company.com` (role-based) | ❌ No | Skip role-based, reject in validate_input.py |

### Override per founder con personal email

Se utente vuole forzare invio a personal email founder (es. Mario Rossi `m.rossi@gmail.com` per Acme.io):

```yaml
gdpr:
  reject_b2c_personal_email: false  # override esplicito
  manual_review_personal_email: true  # almeno flag for review
```

Subagent in questo caso flag warning ma procede.

## 6. Retention 12 mesi

### Default policy

- Lead in CRM: 12 mesi post-last-contact
- Reset al re-engagement (reply, click, opt-in form)
- Post-12 mesi senza engagement: archive lead, no further outreach

### Implementation

`/outbound-orchestrator` non gestisce retention auto (responsability CRM). Ma flag in report:

```markdown
## Retention review

I seguenti lead sono stati contattati >12 mesi fa senza engagement:

- mario.rossi@company.com (last contact 2025-04-30)
- ...

Consigliato: archive in CRM o re-LIA prima di nuovo outreach.
```

### Re-LIA su lead "stale"

Se utente vuole ri-targetare lead >12 mesi:
- LIA nuovo per nuova campagna (re-document interest)
- Nuovo opt-out info nel primo email
- Source updated (cosa è cambiato per giustificare re-engage)

## 7. Article 9 reject — sensitive data

GDPR Article 9: special category data BANNED unless explicit consent.

```
- Health data
- Racial or ethnic origin
- Political opinions
- Religious or philosophical beliefs
- Trade union membership
- Genetic data
- Biometric data
- Sex life or sexual orientation
- Convictions and offences
```

### Schema validation reject

Skill `gdpr-opt-out` parse lead JSON e check fields:

```python
ARTICLE_9_KEYWORDS = {
    "health": ["medical", "patient", "diagnosis", "disease"],
    "race": ["ethnicity", "ancestry"],
    "political": ["political party", "voting"],
    "religion": ["religious", "faith", "denomination"],
    "sexual": ["orientation", "preference"],
    "biometric": ["fingerprint", "facial recognition"]
}

def reject_article_9(lead_json):
    issues = []
    for category, keywords in ARTICLE_9_KEYWORDS.items():
        for field, value in flatten(lead_json).items():
            if any(kw in str(value).lower() for kw in keywords):
                issues.append({"field": field, "category": category, "value_excerpt": str(value)[:50]})
    return issues
```

Se `issues` non vuoto → REJECT lead, log warning.

## 8. B2C personal email reject

### Default policy

`reject_b2c_personal_email: true` (config default).

### Personal email domains list

```python
PERSONAL_EMAIL_DOMAINS = {
    "gmail.com", "googlemail.com",
    "yahoo.com", "yahoo.it", "yahoo.co.uk",
    "hotmail.com", "hotmail.it", "outlook.com", "live.com", "msn.com",
    "icloud.com", "me.com", "mac.com",
    "libero.it", "alice.it", "tin.it", "virgilio.it",
    "tiscali.it", "fastwebnet.it",
    "aol.com", "protonmail.com", "tutanota.com",
    "orange.fr", "wanadoo.fr", "free.fr",
    "web.de", "gmx.de", "t-online.de"
}

def is_personal_email(email):
    domain = email.split("@")[1].lower()
    return domain in PERSONAL_EMAIL_DOMAINS
```

Reject in `validate_input.py` se `is_personal_email(lead.email)` AND `gdpr.reject_b2c_personal_email = true`.

## 9. Output skill `gdpr-opt-out`

```python
def check_compliance(leads, icp, gdpr_config):
    """Returns dict with compliant + excluded + footer."""
    compliant = []
    excluded = []

    for lead in leads:
        # Article 9 check
        article_9_issues = reject_article_9(lead)
        if article_9_issues:
            excluded.append({"lead": lead, "reason": "article_9_sensitive", "issues": article_9_issues})
            continue

        # Personal email check
        if gdpr_config["reject_b2c_personal_email"] and is_personal_email(lead["email"]):
            excluded.append({"lead": lead, "reason": "b2c_personal_email", "domain": lead["email"].split("@")[1]})
            continue

        # Suppression check
        if is_in_suppression_list(lead["email"]):
            excluded.append({"lead": lead, "reason": "in_suppression_list"})
            continue

        # Role-based check (ridondante con validate_input.py, defense in depth)
        if is_role_based(lead["email"]):
            excluded.append({"lead": lead, "reason": "role_based_email"})
            continue

        compliant.append(lead)

    footer_html = generate_footer(icp, gdpr_config) if icp.geo_eu_detected else generate_footer_us_only(gdpr_config)
    lia_doc_path = ensure_lia_documented(icp, gdpr_config)

    return {
        "compliant_leads": compliant,
        "excluded": excluded,
        "footer_html": footer_html,
        "lia_doc_path": lia_doc_path,
        "summary": {
            "total_input": len(leads),
            "compliant_count": len(compliant),
            "excluded_count": len(excluded),
            "exclusion_breakdown": {reason: count for reason, count in...}
        }
    }
```

## 10. Common compliance pitfall

| Pitfall | Risk | Mitigation |
|---------|------|------------|
| LIA non documentato pre-send | Sanzione Garante (max €20M o 4% revenue) | Hard gate in subagent: no execute senza LIA file |
| Suppression non sync cross-stack | Lead riceve email da campagna altra | Suppression sync mandatory, retry fail audit |
| Footer monolingua su lead EU | Privacy violation Italia | Footer bilingue auto se EU detected |
| Personal email B2C contattato | Privacy violation + reputation | Reject default `reject_b2c_personal_email: true` |
| Article 9 inadvertent (es. lead "Director Cardiology") | Sanzione + brand damage | Schema validation reject keywords |
| Retention >12mo sostanzialmente | "right to be forgotten" violation | Report retention review, archive |
| No Privacy Policy linkata | Non-compliance art. 13/14 | Footer enforces `{privacy_url}` |
| Source non documentato | Non-compliance art. 14(2)(f) | `_meta.source` field mandatory in lead JSON |
